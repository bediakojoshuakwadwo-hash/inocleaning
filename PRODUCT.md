# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: business decision-makers in Kumasi (Ghana) — shop owners, office managers, landlords — booking recurring or one-off commercial cleaning. Secondary: homeowners booking home, sofa/carpet, post-construction, or after-event cleaning. Visitors typically arrive intent-driven (found via TikTok, word of mouth, or search near KNUST) and want fast reassurance that the crew is trustworthy before committing to book.

## Product Purpose

A marketing site for Ino Cleaning Services that converts local visitors into bookings via the `/book` flow. It exists to make a small, real local cleaning business look credible and easy to trust online, and to make booking a cleaning as low-friction as a phone call. Success is a completed booking or a phone/TikTok contact, not pageviews.

## Brand Personality

Warm and friendly local business — approachable and personable rather than corporate-cold, but still professional enough to earn trust for office/commercial contracts. Voice: plain-spoken, confident, neighborly. Emotional goal: "these are real people I can trust in my space," not "generic franchise service."

## Anti-references

- Generic corporate/franchise cleaning-company templates (stock blue-and-white B2B SaaS look) — should feel like a specific, known local business, not an interchangeable template.
- Cold, sterile, overly clinical "industrial cleaning" aesthetics.
- Anonymous global feel — the Kumasi/KNUST location, Ghana contact channels (phone, TikTok), and local business hours should read as genuinely local, not templated.

## Design Principles

- **Warm reliability**: every screen reassures (vetted crew, guarantees, real contact info) while staying personable, never corporate-stiff.
- **Booking is the product**: the `/book` flow gets the same design rigor as the marketing pages — friction there costs real business, not just engagement.
- **Business-first, residential-friendly**: lead messaging and imagery toward office/shop/landlord decision-makers as the primary audience, without alienating home-cleaning customers who are still core business.
- **Built for Kumasi, not generic**: reflect the specific local context (KNUST-area location, Ghana phone/WhatsApp/TikTok) rather than template polish that could belong to any city.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Standard WCAG AA baseline: body text ≥4.5:1 contrast, large text ≥3:1, full keyboard navigation, readable type sizes, `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives for all motion.
